## ⚡ About Me

I am a **Computer Science Student at COMSATS University** engineering software at the triple intersection of **Production Mobile Development**, **User Experience (UX) Design**, and **Network Infrastructure**.

- 📱 **Mobile Architecture**: Building resilient, production-ready cross-platform applications with **Flutter/Dart**, native **Java/C#**, state management pipelines (Bloc/Provider), and clean decoupled architecture.
- 🎨 **User Experience Engineering**: Certified in **Google UX Design**, transforming user research and high-fidelity Figma prototypes into fluid, accessible, and intuitive UI conversion pipelines.
- 🌐 **Network Systems & Infrastructure**: Hands-on expertise in network protocol analysis, routing & switching topology architecture (**CCNA Junior Networking Technician** foundation), and distributed systems performance.
- 🚀 **Flagship Software**: Engineering flagship platforms **Vybin** (real-time chat with ZegoCloud audio/video integration) and **SnapGrade** (semantic automated assessment engine).
